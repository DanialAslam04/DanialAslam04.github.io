03Approach

How I Build Agents

The agent reasons freely inside a boundary it cannot widen. Policy decides what commits on its own and what waits for a person — so routine writes are never slowed down, and the ones that matter are never silent.

Underneath: Vertex AI and Gemini for inference, routed through LiteLLM; BigQuery vector search backing RAG and semantic discovery; GKE for orchestration and Redis for caching and rate limiting.
